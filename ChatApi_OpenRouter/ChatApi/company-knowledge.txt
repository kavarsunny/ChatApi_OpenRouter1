#### 🍛 LUNCH POLICIES & GUIDELINES
*   **Booking Deadline**: Lunch tokens must be booked on the TatvaTech intranet portal (canteen.TatvaTech.com) or at the reception desk by **10:30 AM** daily. 
*   **Jain Food Options**: Jain-friendly versions of the daily rotating thali (no root vegetables, onions, or garlic) are available for all meals. Jain orders must be placed by **6:00 PM on the previous day**.
*   **Service Model**: Fully self-service. Present your digital or physical token at the service counter on the 4th floor to collect your plate.
*   **Cleanliness & Waste**: Return all used plates, bowls, and cutlery to the dishwashing return station. Do not leave plates on the dining tables.
*   **Visitor Lunch Policy**: Non-employees can purchase guest lunch coupons at the reception desk for **₹100**.

### 📖 CANTEEN RESTAURANT MENU & INGREDIENT LIST

#### 🌅 MORNING BREAKFASTS (8:00 AM - 10:00 AM)
*   **Poha** (₹30)
    *   *Ingredients*: Flattened rice (poha), mustard seeds, curry leaves, onions, green chillies, roasted peanuts, turmeric, fresh coriander, lemon juice.
*   **Methi Thepla** (₹35)
    *   *Ingredients*: Whole wheat flour, fresh fenugreek leaves (methi), ginger-garlic paste, sesame seeds, yoghurt, turmeric, red chilli powder, oil. Served with curd and sweet-sour pickle.
*   **Idli Sambhar** (₹40)
    *   *Ingredients*: Fermented rice and black gram (urad dal) batter, steamed. Served with Sambhar (tuvar dal, drumstick, pumpkin, tamarind, curry leaves, sambhar masala) and Coconut Chutney (grated coconut, green chillies, ginger, mustard seeds).
*   **Fafda Gathiya** (₹35)
    *   *Ingredients*: Gram flour (besan), papad khar, carom seeds (ajwain), black pepper, oil. Served with warm sweet-sour besan kadhi, raw papaya salad, and fried chillies.
*   **Upma** (₹30)
    *   *Ingredients*: Semolina (suji), onions, green chillies, ginger, mustard seeds, curry leaves, split black gram (urad dal), carrots, ghee. Served with coconut chutney.

#### 🍛 LUNCH COMPOSITIONS (12:30 PM - 2:30 PM)
*   **Gujarati Thali** (₹80)
    *   *Roti*: Whole wheat flour, ghee.
    *   *Dry Sabji*: Potato & Ivy gourd (Aloo-Tindora) with dry spices.
    *   *Rasa Sabji*: Gujarati style tomato-potato curry.
    *   *Dal*: Tuvar dal, jaggery, tomatoes, chillies, mustard seeds, curry leaves.
    *   *Rice*: Steamed Basmati rice.
    *   *Sides*: Fresh salad, mixed pickle, roasted papad, and fresh salted buttermilk (Chaas).
*   **Punjabi Thali** (₹90)
    *   *Paneer Sabji*: Cottage cheese (paneer) in rich tomato-onion-cashew gravy, butter, cream, kasuri methi, garam masala.
    *   *Veg Kolhapuri*: Mixed vegetables (beans, peas, carrot, cauliflower) in spicy red gravy.
    *   *Roti*: 2 pieces of whole wheat tandoori flatbread with butter.
    *   *Dal Tadka*: Yellow lentils (moong/masoor) tempered with cumin, garlic, dry red chilli, and ghee.
    *   *Jeera Rice*: Basmati rice tossed with cumin seeds and ghee.
    *   *Sides*: Sliced onions, lemon wedge, and buttermilk (Chaas).
*   **Kathiyawadi Thali** (₹85)
    *   *Bajra Rotla*: Pearl millet flour flatbread cooked over clay griddle, smeared with fresh white butter.
    *   *Sev Tameta Sabji*: Sweet-sour-spicy tomato gravy topped with crispy gram flour noodles (sev).
    *   *Ringan Bharta*: Roasted eggplant mashed with spring onions, garlic, ginger, green chillies, coriander.
    *   *Khichdi*: Slow-cooked rice and split moong dal, served with a dollop of pure ghee.
    *   *Kadhi*: Yoghurt, gram flour (besan), green chillies, ginger, fenugreek seeds.
*   **Veg Biryani** (₹80)
    *   *Ingredients*: Basmati rice layered with mixed vegetables (beans, peas, carrot, potato) marinated in yoghurt and spices, mint, coriander, fried onions, saffron, whole cardamom, cinnamon. Served with onion raita and papad.
*   **Chole Bhature** (₹80)
    *   *Chole*: Chickpeas (kabuli chana) slow-cooked in spicy gravy of tea-decoction, onions, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, special chole masala.
    *   *Bhature*: Refined flour (maida), semolina (suji), yoghurt, baking powder, deep-fried. Served with pickled green chillies, onions, and lemon.

#### 🍔 AFTERNOON HI-TEA FAST FOOD (4:30 PM - 5:30 PM)
*   **Vada Pav** (₹40)
    *   *Vada*: Mashed potatoes spiced with garlic, ginger, green chillies, dipped in chickpea batter and deep-fried.
    *   *Pav*: Soft yeast bread rolls. Served with green mint-coriander chutney and spicy dry garlic-coconut chutney.
*   **Veg Cheese Grilled Sandwich** (₹50)
    *   *Ingredients*: White bread, sliced cucumber, tomatoes, potatoes, capsicum, processed cheese slices, green chutney, butter, sandwich masala, grilled.
*   **Aloo Samosa** (₹30)
    *   *Ingredients*: Refined flour (maida) crust with carom seeds (ajwain) and ghee. Stuffing of boiled potatoes, green peas, coriander seeds, ginger, green chillies, garam masala, raisins, deep-fried. Served with sweet dates-tamarind chutney.
*   **Kutchi Dabeli** (₹40)
    *   *Ingredients*: Sweet bun filled with mashed potatoes mixed with special dabeli masala, sweet tamarind chutney, spicy garlic chutney, roasted spiced peanuts, fresh pomegranate seeds, sev.
*   **Sev Khamani** (₹40)
    *   *Ingredients*: Crumbled chana dal khaman, tempered with mustard seeds, green chillies, ginger, garlic, sesame seeds, topped with pomegranate seeds, sev, fresh coriander.
